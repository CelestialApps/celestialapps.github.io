## 2. Information Collection and Storage

### A. Local Game Data (On-Device Storage)
**Dog Runner: Jack's Adventure** stores all player progression and preference data locally on your device using standard HTML5 LocalStorage and native Android storage. This includes:
* High scores and record distance statistics
* Unlocked and equipped Runes (Life Rune, Boomerang Capacity Rune, Turbo Score Rune)
* Audio preferences (sound effects and background music toggles)
* Selected interface language (from the 22 supported languages)

We do **not** transmit, store, or maintain this gameplay data on our own remote servers. There is no user registration, login, or account creation required to play the game.

### B. Automatically Collected Data & Third-Party SDKs
To provide free gameplay, the Application integrates the official **Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob)** provided by Google LLC. These services may automatically collect certain device and usage information, including:
* Android Advertising ID (AAID / Google Advertising ID)
* IP address (used for coarse geographic location like country or region for ad delivery)
* Device model, screen resolution, operating system version, and system language
* Diagnostic, performance, and ad delivery metrics

## 4. How to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads

You have full control over the Advertising ID on your Android device:
1. Open your device **Settings**.
2. Navigate to **Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads** depending on your Android version).
3. Tap **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.
4. You can also toggle **Opt out of Ads Personalization**.

Uninstalling the Application immediately ceases all future data processing on your device.
