## 2. Information Collection and Storage

### A. Local Game Data (On-Device Storage)
**Nebula Vanguard** is an offline-first game. All player progression and preference data is stored locally on your device using Android `SharedPreferences`. This includes:

* High score, best wave reached, total runs and total kills
* Salvage currency and permanent hangar upgrade levels
* Selected ship and unlocked ships
* Audio preferences (sound and music), haptics, reduced flash and reduced shake settings
* Selected interface language (system default or one of the 22 supported languages)
* Daily run scores and daily attempt counters
* Tutorial completion flag

We do **not** transmit, store, or maintain this gameplay data on our own remote servers. There is no user registration, login, or account creation required to play the game, and there are no cloud saves. Deleting the Application removes this local data from your device.

### B. Automatically Collected Data & Third-Party SDKs
The Application integrates the official **Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob)**, provided by Google LLC, to show an optional rewarded advertisement. When the ad SDK loads an advertisement, it may automatically collect:

* Android Advertising ID (AAID / Google Advertising ID)
* IP address (used to derive coarse geographic location, such as country or region, for ad delivery and fraud prevention)
* Device model, manufacturer, screen size and resolution, operating system version
* System language, network type, mobile operator
* Ad delivery diagnostics: impressions, clicks, reward completion, performance and error metrics

We do not receive this data in identifiable form ourselves; it is processed by Google as described in its own policies. We receive only aggregated revenue and performance reports from AdMob.

### C. What We Do NOT Collect
The Application does **not** access or collect:

* Your name, email address, phone number or contacts
* Precise location (GPS)
* Photos, camera, microphone or files on your device
* Health, biometric or financial data
* Accounts, passwords or payment information (the game has no in-app purchases)
* Analytics profiles, crash reports or advertising profiles of our own (we use no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs)

## 4. Advertising and Your Choices

### A. Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads
Depending on your region and your choices, Google may serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. Where the applicable law requires consent for personalized advertising (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Application requests that consent through Google's certified consent management tools before serving personalized ads; if consent is refused or not yet given, only non-personalized ads are requested.

### B. How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads
You have full control over the Advertising ID on your Android device:

1. Open your device **Settings**.
2. Navigate to **Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, depending on your Android version).
3. Tap **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.
4. You can also toggle **Opt out of Ads Personalization**.

### C. No Sale of Personal Data
We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We do not share data with data brokers. Advertising data is shared only with Google as the ad provider described above.

### D. Removal
Uninstalling the Application immediately stops all future processing initiated by the Application on your device.
