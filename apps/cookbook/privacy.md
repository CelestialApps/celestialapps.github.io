## 2. Information Collection and Storage

### A. Local Application Data (On-Device Storage)

**Cookbook** stores your preferences and recipe data locally on your device using Android `SharedPreferences` and local SQLite database storage. This includes:

* Saved favorite recipes (Bookmarks / Favorites list)
* Selected ingredient filters and pantry search criteria
* Dietary preferences and filter toggles (e.g., Vegetarian, Pork-Free, Beef-Free, Dairy-Free)
* Local purchase verification status (ad-free premium entitlement)

We do **not** transmit, store, or maintain this data on our own remote servers. There is no user registration, account creation, or login required to use the application. The entire core recipe collection and database function fully offline.

### B. Automatically Collected Data & Third-Party SDKs

To provide a free utility application supported by advertising and to process optional digital purchases, Cookbook integrates third-party software development kits (SDKs) provided by Google. These services may automatically collect certain device and usage telemetry, including:

* Android Advertising ID (AAID / Google Advertising ID)
* IP address (used to determine approximate coarse geographic location for regional ad delivery)
* Device model, operating system version, and unique hardware identifiers
* Performance, diagnostic, and crash metrics

## 5. How to Control Ads and Opt-Out

You have full control over advertising preferences and identifiers on your Android device:

1. **In-App Ad Removal:** You can make a one-time in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") directly within the application to permanently disable all advertisements.
2. **Resetting or Deleting Advertising ID:**
   * Open your device **Settings**.
   * Navigate to **Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads** depending on your Android version).
   * Tap **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.
   * You can also toggle **Opt out of Ads Personalization** where available.

Uninstalling the application immediately ceases all future data processing on your device.
