## 2. Information Collection and Storage

### A. Local Application Data (On-Device Storage)

**Weapons** is a comprehensive military encyclopedia. It stores your preferences and saved content strictly locally on your device using Android `SharedPreferences`. This includes:

* Saved favorite weapons and equipment (Bookmarks / Favorites list)
* Selected interface language preference (e.g., Russian, English)
* Scroll positions and UI display zoom preferences

We do **not** transmit, store, or maintain this data on remote servers. There is no user registration, account creation, or login required to use the application. The entire core encyclopedia catalog, including all articles, specifications, and images, operates completely offline.

### B. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and offer the application for free, **Weapons** integrates third-party software development kits (SDKs) provided by Google. The free version displays banner advertisements powered by **Google AdMob**.

These services may automatically collect certain device and usage telemetry, including:
* Android Advertising ID (AAID / Google Advertising ID)
* IP address (used to determine approximate coarse geographic location for regional ad delivery)
* Device model, operating system version, and unique hardware identifiers
* Performance, diagnostic, and crash metrics

Third-party advertising providers process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:
* **Google Play Services:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Google AdMob:** [https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en)

## 3. How to Control Ads and Opt-Out

You have full control over advertising preferences and identifiers on your Android device:

* **Resetting or Deleting Advertising ID:**
  * Open your device **Settings**.
  * Navigate to **Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads** depending on your Android version).
  * Tap **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.
  * You can also toggle **Opt out of Ads Personalization** where available.

Uninstalling the application immediately ceases all future data processing on your device.
